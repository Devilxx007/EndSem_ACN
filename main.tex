\section*{How to read these notes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each chapter covers one paper: what problem it solves, how the system works, and the features to remember. Evaluation numbers are left out unless they help explain a design.
  \item \key{Blue bold} marks a key term. Blue boxes hold the ideas most likely to be asked.
  \item \textcolor{red!60!black}{\textbf{Red boxes}} hold the handwritten margin notes from the annotated PDFs, close to the passage they annotate.
  \item Figures are reproduced from the papers wherever a picture explains the mechanism.
\end{itemize}
